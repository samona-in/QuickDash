# Build Brief: Premium Landing Page for an On-Demand Blue-Collar Services Marketplace

You are an expert product designer, UX designer, and senior frontend engineer.

Build a **high-end, modern, premium landing page** for a new Indian consumer marketplace that connects customers with nearby skilled blue-collar professionals.

The product is similar conceptually to:

* Uber → customers request nearby drivers
* DoorDash → customers request services/products
* Urban Company → customers book local professionals
* Rapido → customers connect with nearby service providers

But the product is intended to be a broader **on-demand marketplace for skilled local workers**.

The landing page must make the product understandable within **5–10 seconds**.

The user should immediately understand:

1. What the product does
2. Who it is for
3. What problems it solves
4. How booking works
5. What types of services are available
6. Why customers should trust it
7. How professionals can join

---

# 1. PRODUCT CONCEPT

The basic flow is:

Customer has a problem.

For example:

> "My AC isn't cooling."

They open the platform.

They select:

> AC Repair

The platform finds nearby available professionals.

The customer can see:

* Name
* Profile
* Rating
* Experience
* Jobs completed
* Distance
* Estimated price
* Availability

The customer selects a professional and books them.

The professional receives the job, accepts it, travels to the customer, completes the work, and receives payment.

The customer can then rate the professional.

The fundamental product loop is:

**Need → Find → Book → Get it done**
